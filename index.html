What to Expect

Friendly over-the-board games in a cozy library setting.

Casual & Low-Stress

No ratings, no tournament pressure, relaxed untimed or friendly rapid games.

An Excuse to Get Out

A warm Sunday morning ritual to beat cabin fever during Vermont winters.

All Skill Levels Welcome

From seasoned players to those who haven’t played across an actual board in years. As long as you know the basic rules of the game, you’re welcome to play.

Drop-In Friendly

10:00 AM to 12:00 PM. Drop in for 30 minutes or stay the whole two hours.

Logistics & FAQ

Everything you need to know before dropping by.

♘ Cost & Registration
Free to attend. No membership fees or advance sign-up required—just show up ready for a friendly game.
♖ Where Inside the Library?
Meets in the library’s designated community/quiet room at 180 Market Street.
♗ Equipment
Boards and sets provided (or bring your favorite set and clock if you like).
♕ Winter Weather Policy
Follows South Burlington Public Library operating status—if the library closes for severe winter weather, chess is off.
